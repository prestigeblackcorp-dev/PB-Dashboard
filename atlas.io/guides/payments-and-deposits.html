Payments & pricing

Payments, deposits and refunds

Atlas connects to Stripe or Square, so money moves through your own payment account. Payments, deposit holds and refunds all attach to the booking, so your books stay clean.

Connect your payment provider

You get paid directly through your own Stripe or Square account — Atlas never sits between you and your money, and there are no per-booking fees from us.

  1. Open Settings and choose Payments.
  2. Connect Stripe or Square in a few clicks.
  3. Payments from that point flow into your account.

Refundable security deposits

A deposit hold protects you against damage or late return without charging the customer up front for something that may never happen.

Atlas places a refundable hold at booking and releases it once the item is back and checked over.

Tip: Size the deposit to the item, not the rental length — the risk is the item, whether it is out for a day or a week.

Refunds and disputes

Issue a full or partial refund right from the booking, and the record shows exactly what was charged, held, released and refunded.

If you need to keep part of a deposit for damage, you can — and the booking documents why.

Everything ties back to the booking

Because every charge, hold and refund is attached to its booking, you can always answer 'what happened with this rental?' in one place. That is what keeps your end-of-month reconciliation short.
