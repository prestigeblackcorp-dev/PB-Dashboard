Domain questions, answered

Where can I buy a domain name?+
You can buy a domain name directly here at Atlas Rental.io. Search any name above, see the real yearly price, and register it with free WHOIS privacy and full DNS control — no rental account required.
How do I buy a domain name?+
Search the name you want, pick an available domain, create a free Atlas account, and complete secure checkout. Your domain is registered with WHOIS privacy included and appears in your dashboard right away.
Do I need an Atlas rental account to buy a domain?+
No. A domain-only Atlas account is free and gives you full DNS management for your domain, even if you never use the rental dashboard.
Can customers book on my own domain?+
Yes. When you register through Atlas we point your domain at your Atlas booking site automatically and issue the SSL for you, so customers can book, pay and sign at your own address right away. The booking website itself is included on the Enterprise plan and up, or a $19/month add-on (or one-time $199 build) on the Starter and Pro plans — the domain is billed separately, once a year, at the price shown before you buy.
Can I manage my own DNS records?+
Yes. Every Atlas domain comes with full DNS control — A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT and more — plus free WHOIS privacy, all from one dashboard.
Is WHOIS privacy included?+
Yes, free on every domain. Your personal contact details stay off the public WHOIS record at no extra cost.
How much does a domain cost?+
It depends on the extension (.com, .io, .co and so on). Search your domain above to see the exact yearly price before you buy. Domains renew yearly.
Can I use my domain with a website or email?+
Absolutely. Point it at any website host or email provider using your DNS records — or connect it to your Atlas Rental.io booking site in a click.
