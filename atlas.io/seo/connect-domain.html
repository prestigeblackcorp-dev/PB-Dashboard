Connecting takes three steps

No code. Most operators finish in a few minutes and the domain goes live shortly after.

1

Enter your domain

In your Atlas dashboard, type the domain you already own and choose “connect a domain I have.”

2

Add the records

Atlas shows the exact DNS records to paste at your registrar. Copy them in and save — that is the whole technical part.

3

Go live

Atlas verifies the records and issues your certificate. Your booking site is live on your own address, usually within minutes to a few hours.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a domain I already bought somewhere else?+
Yes. Atlas works with a domain registered at any registrar — GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google, Cloudflare, Squarespace and the rest. You point it at your Atlas booking site with the records Atlas shows you, and reservations come in on your own web address.
How do I point my domain at my Atlas booking site?+
In your dashboard, enter the domain you own. Atlas shows you the exact DNS records to add at your registrar, then verifies them for you. Most domains connect within minutes to a few hours once the records are saved.
Does connecting my own domain cost extra?+
Connecting a domain you already own is included with your booking website. The website is included on the Enterprise plan and up, or a $19/month add-on (or a one-time $199 build) on the Starter and Pro plans. You keep paying your registrar its normal yearly renewal for the domain itself.
Will connecting my website break my email?+
No. Atlas points the website records at your booking site and leaves your mail (MX) records alone, so an existing mailbox on your domain keeps working exactly as it did.
Do I even need a domain to take bookings?+
No — every account gets a free Atlas web address so you can take bookings right away. A custom domain makes your business look established and keeps customers on your own name. You can connect one you own, or register a new one from inside Atlas.
