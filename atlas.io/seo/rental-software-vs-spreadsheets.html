Five things a spreadsheet will never do

Each one is a booking lost, a deposit missed or an hour wasted -- every week.

Stop a double-booking

A cell cannot check availability before you say yes. Atlas holds every item's calendar and refuses a clashing date, so you never hand the same unit to two customers.

Take and release deposits

A spreadsheet cannot charge a card or hold a security deposit. Atlas collects a reserve payment to lock the dates and places a refundable hold you release in a click.

Get the contract signed

Chasing a PDF by email, unsigned, is how disputes start. Atlas sends your agreement for e-signature and stores the signed copy on the booking.

Remind customers automatically

No formula sends a pickup reminder or a return nudge. Atlas fires them on their own, so no-shows and late returns drop without you lifting a finger.

Show real profit per item

SUM() is not a P&L. Atlas tracks revenue, fees and payouts per item and shows live analytics, so you know what actually earns and what just sits.
